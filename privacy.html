Privacy Policy for Clean Habits

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Clean Habits (the “App”) is developed by Elena Lapina. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Information stored on your device

Clean Habits stores your settings, cleaning plans, tasks, and progress locally on your device. This information is used to provide the App’s features and is not transmitted to us.

The App does not require you to create an account. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, the App uses local notifications scheduled on your device. You can change your notification preferences in the App or in your device settings.

Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use that information to respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive information unless it is necessary for us to help you.

Deleting your information

You can reset your data using the settings in the App. You can also remove the App from your device. Deleting the App normally removes its locally stored data; copies may remain in device backups managed by you or Apple.

Third-party services

Clean Habits does not use third-party analytics or advertising services. Apple may process information when you download or use the App Store, according to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App or its privacy practices change. The current version will be available on this page, and the effective date will be updated when appropriate.

Contact and support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, contact Elena Lapina at selap.dev@gmail.com.
